My research

During my research career I've integrated evidence from living organisms and thenfossil record, through the joint application of of an array of state-of-the-art and traditional techniques in palaeobiology, such as polished blocks, palaeohistology, X-ray diffraction analysis, EDS mapping, etc.

This path have lead me to participate in different porjects, and publish my findings in different scientific journals and palaeontological conferences, both national and international
